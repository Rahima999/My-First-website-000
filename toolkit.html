Welcome to My Prompt Toolkit ✨

These ready-to-use prompts can help students find information, understand difficult topics, research effectively, and generate ideas.

🔎 1. Find Information

Use this when you need reliable information about a topic.

"Explain [TOPIC] using simple and accurate information. Give me the key facts, important terms, examples, and reliable sources I can use to learn more."

📚 2. Study a Difficult Topic

Use this when you don't understand something.

"Teach me [TOPIC] like I am a beginner. Explain it step by step using simple language. Give me an easy example and then ask me 5 questions to check my understanding."

📝 3. Improve My Writing

Use this to improve your writing without losing your own ideas.

"Improve the grammar, clarity, and structure of the following text. Keep my original meaning and writing style. Also explain the main mistakes I made."

⚖️ 4. Compare Two Things

Useful for research and assignments.

"Compare [TOPIC A] and [TOPIC B]. Explain their similarities, differences, advantages, disadvantages, and real-world uses. Present the information in a clear table."

💡 5. Generate Ideas

Use this when you need creative ideas for a project.

"Give me 10 creative ideas for a student project about [TOPIC]. Make the ideas practical, original, affordable, and suitable for a school or college student."

🎤 6. Prepare a Presentation

Use this to organize a presentation.

"Create a clear presentation outline about [TOPIC]. Include an introduction, 5 main points, examples, a conclusion, and a short opening and closing speech."

🧠 7. Research Assistant

Use this when starting research.

"Help me research [TOPIC]. First give me the main questions I should investigate. Then suggest useful keywords, important subtopics, and reliable types of sources I should look for."
